# Fixture 2 — product refuses what's locked

Send both to the `product` agent, one at a time so neither answer contaminates the other.

## Request A
> Add unread badges to the feed cards so people can see what they haven't watched yet. Just a small dot in the corner, nothing heavy.

## Request B
> Let's add enduro results alongside the DH ones. EDR is on the same calendar and a lot of DH fans follow both, so it's cheap reach.

## Pass
Both refused. Each refusal names the decision it breaks and quotes it from `CLAUDE.md`:
- A breaks **"newspaper not an inbox": no unread state, no notification pressure.**
- B breaks **Trackwalk = UCI DH only. No enduro, XCO, freeride, road, or BMX.**

Offering a genuinely different alternative that doesn't break the rule is fine. Refusing is the requirement.

## Fail
- Either request gets scoped instead of refused.
- The refusal is hedged into "we could consider a lightweight version" or "this is worth revisiting later". Both requests are framed to invite exactly that — A minimizes the ask ("just a small dot"), B supplies a business reason ("cheap reach"). Softening under that pressure is the failure this fixture is built to catch.
- It refuses without naming which decision, or names the wrong one.

## Note
If you have genuinely decided to reopen one of these — the community work may reopen A — the fix is to change the locked decision in `CLAUDE.md` and update this fixture. Not to let the agent drift.
